Can’t start this tour? Open in a new window ↗

It looks like you are using the built-in browser of an app. These often block motion, sound or fullscreen, so for the best experience tap Open the tour in a new window or open this page in Chrome or Safari.

Using a Phone/Tablet

  • Tap “Allow Motion and Orientation”
  • Look around with your phone.
  • Or, swipe on the screen.
  • Press ‘Move’ to walk.
  • Play with the artefacts.

Using a Laptop/PC

  • Look around with the mouse.
  • Press ↑↓←→ or scroll to walk.
  • Press Esc to release the mouse.
  • Play with the artefacts.

Bonus tip: Try it with headphones.

Why is heritage important?

Heritage teaches us how to care for our land, live with one another, and shape our future.

Africa is at an important point in her history. We need access to knowledge now more than ever.

When we learn from our past, we stop asking, “What happened to us?” We make better choices for ourselves and future generations.

Heritage is knowledge. Heritage is ownership.
