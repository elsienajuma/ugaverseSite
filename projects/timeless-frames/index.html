The Timeless Frames story

A first look at the exhibition

Before the Timeless Frames exhibition, Ssenyonyi Derrick wanted to give people an idea of what it would feel like to walk through it, through a virtual gallery.

Guests tried it on VR headsets at the Timeless Frames press conference, and later on at the photo exhibition on 20 September 2024, at Kampala Serena Hotel.
