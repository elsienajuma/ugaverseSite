Ugaverse - a creative studio

We bring Ugandan stories to the world, the way they are meant to be told.

  • Virtual tours
  • 360° films/video
  • Interactive simulations

Designed and developed by Ugandans, from idea to experience.

Our work is defined by 3 rules.

  1. 01

    Context

    It must be tailored to the local context.

  2. 02

    Quality

    It must be the best quality we can make.

  3. 03

    Accessibility

    It must be easy for people to open and use.

How we work

The process works in 3 simple steps.

  1. Visit

    We visit the place, then measure and scan it.

  2. Build

    We turn it into a 3D model and build the experience.

  3. Share

    We launch it online, or bring it to your event on a headset.
